Where we're headed

From one operator per drone to one operator per fleet.

Today most drones need a person flying them at every step. We're building toward missions the aircraft finish on their own, so a small team can do the work of a large one.

Now

One map for everything

Tasking, positions, alerts and finds from every aircraft in the TAK app your team already carries.

Next

Missions that run themselves

Give the aircraft a task. It plans, flies, searches, keeps clear of other traffic and reports back. The operator approves decisions instead of flying.

The goal

Mass at scale

Many aircraft, and in time ground vehicles too, working one mission together under a handful of operators.
